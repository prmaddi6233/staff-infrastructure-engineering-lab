# Role relevance and ethical scope

This independent portfolio case study is tailored to the requested Senior/Staff Infrastructure
Engineer discussion at Coffee Meets Bagel. It does not describe CMB infrastructure, incidents,
security findings, current staffing or business priorities. No access or endorsement is implied.
The customer journeys are illustrative consumer-app examples, not an observed service map.

| Staff engineering dimension | Reviewable work |
|---|---|
| Technical judgment | Recovery alternatives, explicit assumptions and phased decision gates |
| Reliability | Tested restore rejection paths and proposed journey SLOs |
| Security | Trust boundaries, least-privilege CI and honest checksum limitations |
| Cross-team delivery | Prioritized backlog with owner roles and acceptance evidence |
| Cost stewardship | Reuse of existing projects; cost/recovery trade-offs |
| Communication | Implementation status, reproducible commands and unresolved risks |

Discovery in any real role would begin with product journey owners, platform/application
engineers, security and finance: agree impact tolerance, review existing evidence, identify
failure coupling and choose one narrow pilot. A portfolio design is not a migration mandate.
Historical incident claims from the uploaded starter are intentionally omitted because they
are unnecessary to demonstrate the engineering approach.
